4Langs – Delete Course Data

What data will be deleted?

  • The selected language course
  • Learning progress for the course
  • Completed exercise scores for the course

Your account, email address and any other courses remain unchanged. You can create a new course at any time.

To permanently delete your entire account, please visit our Delete Account page.
